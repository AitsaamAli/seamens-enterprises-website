What Is EcoSoftener D-11?

EcoSoftener D-11 is a lubricating softener formulated for two roles: reducing friction during warp sizing, and delivering a soft hand-feel during fabric finishing. It's offered in a few formulation variants, so a mill isn't locked into one option — Seamens matches the right variant to the fibre and finish being targeted.

Benefits for Sizing

  • High lubrication — less yarn friction against healds and reeds, meaning less heald/reed damage over a production run
  • Less hairiness — the yarn surface is sealed, so fluff doesn't build up on the loom
  • Anti-static — helps on PC/poly and other synthetic-blend counts
  • Better weavability — mills typically see improved loom efficiency with less breakage
  • Soft hand after desizing — fabric doesn't come out stiff once the size is removed

Benefits for Finishing

  • Super-soft hand feel — well suited to towel and bedding, where softness is the main selling point
  • Hydrophilic — water is absorbed quickly and towel absorbency is not compromised
  • Low yellowing — formulated to avoid staining white fabric
  • Economical — a cost-effective option for mills weighing it against imported softener alternatives

Fabrics it works well on: cotton, denim, towel, canvas/drill, PC/PV blends, and bedding/sheeting.

Choosing the Right Variant for Your Fabric

The right variant depends on the fibre and the specific finishing goal — synthetic blends and certain finishing requirements may call for a different variant than standard cotton and denim work. Seamens confirms the right choice during a sample trial on the actual fabric construction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EcoSoftener D-11 used for?
EcoSoftener D-11 is a lubricating softener used in both warp sizing and fabric finishing. In sizing, it lubricates the yarn to reduce heald/reed damage; in finishing, it delivers a super-soft hand feel on fabrics like cotton, denim, towel, canvas/drill, PC/PV blends, and bedding/sheeting.
Is EcoSoftener D-11 available in different variants?
Yes, EcoSoftener D-11 is available in a few formulation variants so a mill can select the one that best suits its fabric and process requirements — Seamens confirms the right choice during a sample trial.
How does EcoSoftener D-11 help in sizing?
In sizing, EcoSoftener D-11 gives high lubrication that reduces yarn friction against healds and reeds, less hairiness by sealing the yarn surface so fluff doesn't build up on the loom, anti-static performance on PC/poly blends, and improved weavability with less breakage.
Does EcoSoftener D-11 cause yellowing on white fabric?
No. EcoSoftener D-11 is formulated to avoid staining, so it does not cause yellowing on white fabric, which makes it suitable for towel, bedding, and other light-shade finishing work.
