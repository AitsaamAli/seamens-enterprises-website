What Is Textile Machine Cleaner Formulated For?

Textile Machine Cleaner is formulated to break down the combined residue of loom oil, fibre lint, and dust that builds up on moving machine parts. It's designed with textile machine surfaces in mind, so it cleans this specific buildup without leaving behind residue that could interfere with fabric quality on the next production run.

What Makes Loom Oil Hard to Remove?

Loom oil is hard to remove because it combines with fibre lint and dust into a compacted residue on moving machine parts. Textile Machine Cleaner is formulated to cut through this specific lint-oil combination, addressing the compacted buildup directly rather than just the oil component alone.

Why this matters for fabric quality: Residue left on moving machine parts can transfer onto fabric, creating quality defects that trace back to a machine-cleaning gap rather than a fabric-processing issue — a costly root cause to trace after the fact.

How Often Should Looms Be Cleaned?

Cleaning TypeTypical Frequency
Thorough cleanWeekly (adjust for high-lint fibres)
Light maintenance cleanDaily

Loom cleaning frequency depends on production volume and fibre type, but many mills schedule thorough cleaning weekly with lighter daily maintenance cleaning, adjusting for higher-lint fibres that require more frequent attention. A cleaning schedule based on actual buildup observation outperforms a fixed generic interval that doesn't account for the specific fibre or production volume.

Is Textile Machine Cleaner Safe on Machine Parts?

Formulated for compatibility: Rubber rollers and coated surfaces need a cleaner chemistry that won't degrade them over repeated use — Textile Machine Cleaner is formulated with this compatibility in mind.

Yes — Textile Machine Cleaner is formulated to be compatible with rubber rollers, coated surfaces, and precision moving parts common on textile machinery, avoiding the material-compatibility risk a mismatched general cleaner can carry, which over time can mean costly repairs or premature part replacement.
