Heavy Duty Industrial Degreasers Pakistan

A complete guide to heavy duty industrial degreasers in Pakistan — selecting the right formulation for machinery, plant floors, engines, and metal surfaces, with correct application methods for safe and effective grease removal.

Key Takeaways

  • Alkaline (water-based) degreasers are the safe choice for most industrial applications — no fire risk, lower toxicity
  • Textile Machine Cleaner is formulated specifically for looms and spinning frames — safe for rubber seals and plastics

  • Seamens supplies industrial degreasers in 5L, 30L, and 210L drums across Pakistan

Industrial grease contamination is one of the most common maintenance challenges in Pakistan's textile mills, food processing plants, engineering workshops, and commercial kitchens. Lubricating oil leaks, cooking fat build-up, hydraulic fluid spills, and grease-packed machinery components all require a degreaser formulated for the specific type of soiling and the surface being cleaned.

Choosing the wrong degreaser — or the wrong dilution — can mean either under-cleaning (leaving grease residue that attracts dirt and causes machinery failures) or over-cleaning (damaging rubber seals, stripping paint, or leaving corrosive residue on metal). Types of Industrial Degreasers

Alkaline Degreasers (Water-Based)

Heavy Duty Degreaser — Seamens' primary industrial degreaser. Alkaline formulation with high-pH surfactant system that saponifies fats, oils, and greases — converting them into water-soluble compounds that can be rinsed away with water. Applications:

  • Plant floors contaminated with hydraulic oil or lubricant drips
  • Machinery exteriors, frames, and housings
  • Commercial kitchen floors, range hoods, and fryer exteriors
  • Vehicle maintenance bays and workshop floors
  • Food processing plant floors and equipment exteriors
  • Loading dock areas and warehouse floors

Textile Machine Cleaner

A specialised degreaser formulated for textile machinery — looms, spinning frames, warping machines, and ancillary equipment. Unlike general industrial degreasers, Textile Machine Cleaner is formulated to be safe on the rubber seals, plastic components, and synthetic rope guides common in textile machines. Removes lubricating oil, size compound, fibre residue, and grease without attacking non-metal components.

Metal Surface Cleaner & Passivator

For precision metal surfaces, fabricated steel components, and ferrous metal parts that require degreasing before painting, coating, or welding. Contains a passivating agent that leaves a thin protective film on ferrous metal surfaces after cleaning — preventing flash rust before the next process step.

Application Guide by Industry

Textile Mills (Lahore, Faisalabad, Karachi)

  • Weekly cleaning of loom frames and under-machine areas: Heavy Duty Degreaser at 1:10 dilution
  • Spinning frame cleaning between doffing cycles: Textile Machine Cleaner at 1:5 dilution
  • Annual plant shutdown deep clean: Heavy Duty Degreaser neat on encrusted areas with 20-minute dwell time

Food Processing Plants

  • Floor cleaning in production areas: Heavy Duty Degreaser at 1:10 — rinse thoroughly before food production resumes
  • Cooking equipment exteriors: Heavy Duty Degreaser at 1:5 on hot surfaces with PPE
  • Note: Food contact surfaces require Food Grade Sanitiser, not Heavy Duty Degreaser

Engineering Workshops

  • Workshop floor degreasing: Heavy Duty Degreaser at 1:5–1:10, scrub and rinse
  • Metal parts degreasing before inspection: Metal Surface Cleaner & Passivator

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the strongest industrial degreaser available in Pakistan?
Heavy Duty Degreaser from Seamens Enterprises is formulated for the most challenging industrial grease removal — machinery encrusted with lubricating grease and carbonised oil, food plant equipment coated with cooking fats, and textile machinery with oil and size compound residue. At neat or low dilution (1:3 to 1:5), it cuts through heavy grease deposits that standard cleaners cannot touch.
What is the difference between alkaline degreaser and solvent degreaser?
Alkaline degreasers work by saponification — converting fats and oils into water-soluble soaps that can be rinsed away. They are water-based, lower-risk, and suitable for most industrial surfaces. Solvent-based degreasers dissolve oils through solvent action and evaporate quickly — used on electrical equipment and precision parts where water is not suitable. Seamens supplies alkaline Heavy Duty Degreaser for most industrial applications.
Can I use heavy duty degreaser on textile machinery?
Yes, but Seamens' Textile Machine Cleaner is specifically formulated for textile machinery — it removes lubricating oil, size compound, fibre residue, and grease without attacking rubber seals, plastic components, or painted surfaces common on looms and spinning frames.
How do you degrease industrial plant floors safely?
Apply Heavy Duty Degreaser at 1:5 to 1:10 dilution to the floor. Allow 10–15 minutes dwell time. Agitate with a stiff broom or scrubber-dryer machine, then mop or rinse clean. Staff should wear rubber gloves, safety goggles, and non-slip footwear. Never leave saponified grease on the floor without rinsing — it creates a severe slip hazard.
