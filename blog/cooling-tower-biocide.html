Cooling Tower Biocide & Algaecide Pakistan

A complete guide to cooling tower biological control in Pakistan — biocides, algaecides, Legionella control, and complete chemical treatment programmes for industrial and commercial cooling towers.

Seamens EnterprisesJune 20267 min read
Key Takeaways
  • Pakistan's hot summers create ideal conditions for rapid bacterial and algal growth in cooling towers
  • Use oxidising + non-oxidising biocides in alternating rotation for best biological control
  • FC-70/90 non-oxidising biocides penetrate biofilm that chlorine cannot reach
  • Water Safe 1020 provides corrosion + scale + dispersion protection in one continuous dose
  • Weekly water testing and monthly specialist review are essential for compliant cooling tower operation

Industrial cooling towers serve as the heat rejection component of air conditioning systems, process chillers, and manufacturing cooling loops across Pakistan. Their warm, open-to-atmosphere water system provides exactly the conditions that bacteria, algae, and biofilm require to thrive — warm water, sunlight, nutrients from make-up water and the atmosphere, and abundant surface area on fill media.

Without a consistent biocide and algaecide programme, cooling tower water rapidly becomes a source of system performance problems (biofilm fouling of heat exchangers) and a potential health risk (Legionella proliferation). Cooling Tower Biocide Programme

Oxidising Biocides

Chlorine-based oxidising biocides are the first line of biological control in cooling towers:

  • Sodium hypochlorite (liquid bleach) — dosed to maintain free chlorine 0.5–1.0 ppm in the circulating water
  • Stabilised bromine — more effective at higher pH (7.5–8.5) than chlorine; used where system pH is naturally high
  • Applied continuously via dosing pump or via shock dosing twice weekly
  • Effective against planktonic bacteria but limited penetration into established biofilm

Non-Oxidising Biocides (FC-70/90)

FC-70 and FC-90 are Seamens Enterprises' non-oxidising biocide products for cooling tower systems:

  • FC-70 — Isothiazolinone-based non-oxidising biocide. Excellent biofilm penetration. Effective against bacteria, algae, and fungi at low dose rates. Dose every 2 weeks as part of alternating biocide programme.
  • FC-90 — Quaternary ammonium compound (QUAT) based non-oxidising biocide. Provides both biocidal action and surfactant properties that help disperse biofilm from fill media surfaces. Alternate with FC-70 to prevent resistance development.

Rotating between FC-70 and FC-90 (alternating fortnightly) prevents biocide resistance from developing and provides broader-spectrum control than either product alone.

Algaecide

Algae in cooling towers grows on fill media surfaces, in the basin, and on splash guards where sunlight can reach. Treatment:

  • Cooling Tower Cleaner for physical cleaning of established algal growth (drain, clean, refill)
  • Algaecide (quaternary ammonium or copper-based, depending on system metallurgy) for ongoing control
  • Basin shading where possible to reduce light-driven algal growth

Scale and Corrosion Treatment

Water Safe 1020

Water Safe 1020 is Seamens Enterprises' multifunctional cooling water treatment product. Dosed continuously to the cooling tower basin at 200–400 ppm, it provides:

  • Corrosion inhibition — For carbon steel, copper alloys, and galvanised steel components in the cooling circuit. Prevents pitting corrosion and general corrosion that reduces heat exchanger tube life.
  • Scale inhibition — Polyphosphate/polymer blend prevents calcium carbonate and other mineral scales from depositing on heat exchanger tubes and fill media.
  • Dispersant action — Keeps suspended solids and corrosion products in suspension for removal by blowdown rather than allowing them to deposit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do cooling towers need biocide treatment in Pakistan?
Cooling towers are warm, wet, nutrient-rich environments ideal for bacterial and algal growth. Untreated water develops biofilm that reduces heat transfer, promotes corrosion, and creates Legionella risk. Pakistan's hot summers (up to 45°C) create particularly favourable conditions for rapid biological growth. Biocide treatment is essential for system efficiency and safety.
What is the difference between oxidising and non-oxidising biocides for cooling towers?
Oxidising biocides (chlorine, bromine) chemically oxidise bacterial cell components — fast-acting but limited biofilm penetration. Non-oxidising biocides (FC-70/90 — isothiazolinones and QUATs) are more persistent and penetrate biofilm effectively. Best practice is to alternate both types — oxidising for broad bacterial control and non-oxidising for biofilm penetration and resistance prevention.
How do you treat algae in a cooling tower?
Cooling tower algae is treated with algaecide added to system water. For severe blooms, the tower must be physically cleaned first (drain, scrub with Cooling Tower Cleaner, rinse) before re-treating with algaecide. Basin shading reduces light-driven algal regrowth significantly.
How often should cooling tower water be tested in Pakistan?
Weekly testing as a minimum during the cooling season. Key parameters: pH (target 7.0–8.5), conductivity/TDS, total hardness, total alkalinity, and bacteria count (dip slide). Monthly specialist testing should include Legionella risk assessment. Seamens provides cooling tower water testing in Lahore and Punjab.
What is Water Safe 1020 and what does it do in a cooling tower?
Water Safe 1020 is Seamens Enterprises' multifunctional cooling water treatment product that combines corrosion inhibitor (for carbon steel, copper, and galvanised steel), scale inhibitor (polyphosphate/polymer blend), and dispersant in one product. Dosed continuously at 200–400 ppm, it protects heat exchanger tubes from corrosion and prevents scale deposition that reduces heat transfer efficiency.
