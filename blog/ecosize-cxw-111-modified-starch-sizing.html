What Is EcoSize-CXW-111?

EcoSize-CXW-111 is a white granular sizing powder supplied in a 25 KG bag or a 100 KG plastic drum. It is German based organic technology, and combines PVA (all kinds) saver polymers, a body enhancer, friction reducer, penetration booster, static control and an anti-foam system. It is ready to use for sizing, and it brings cost and energy savings.

EcoSize CXW-111: technical benefits and dosing

EcoSize CXW-111 (Powder)

This product is German Based Organic Technology having PVA (all kinds) saver polymers, body enhancer, friction reducer, penetration booster, static control and anti-foam system. Ready to use for sizing; no additive (starch, wax etc.) required. Cost saving and energy saving. White granular powder, 25 KG bag or 100 KG plastic drum.

Replacement of all kinds of PVAs and powder chemicals. When you use it, there is no need to add starch, wax, polymers etc. in the size mix, because they are already added in this powder. For some quality counts, small quantities of these items (starch, wax, polymers) can be added.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EcoSize-CXW-111?
EcoSize-CXW-111 is a white granular sizing powder (25 KG bag or 100 KG plastic drum) from Seamens Enterprises: German based organic technology. It combines PVA (all kinds) saver polymers, body enhancer, friction reducer, penetration booster, static control and an anti-foam system, ready to use for sizing.
Do I need to add starch, wax or polymers with EcoSize-CXW-111?
No. EcoSize-CXW-111 replaces all kinds of PVAs and powder chemicals, and starch, wax and polymers are already included in the powder. For some quality counts, small quantities of these items can still be added.
How much PVA does EcoSize-CXW-111 save?
In the comparison supplied by Seamens, PVA consumption is 30% less than PVA BP-317. Indicative doses: 100% cotton 80 kg per 1000 litres against 100 kg for PVA BP-317; PC 80/20 100 kg against 120 kg; PV 65/35 110 kg against 130 kg. Actual results depend on the yarn, machine and process and are confirmed by a trial.
Does it reduce foam and static in the size box?
Per the Seamens comparison, foam in the size box is zero and static is nil, because an anti-foam system and static control are built into the powder.
