Textile Softeners & Finishers ECO-Series Pakistan

A complete guide to ECO-Series textile softeners and finishing agents for Pakistan's textile mills — EcoSoftener D-11, Eco-SilSoftner range, EcoFix-FB and EcoFlame-FR Pro.

Textile finishing is the final wet processing stage that defines how a fabric looks, feels, performs and lasts. Seamens Enterprises supplies ECO-Series textile softeners and finishing agents: German based organic chemical technology, made in Germany, for Pakistan's mills. This guide covers EcoSoftener D-11, the Eco-SilSoftner range, EcoFix-FB and EcoFlame-FR Pro.

EcoSoftener D-11 — Softener for Sizing and Dyeing

EcoSoftener D-11 is a white thick paste, a replacement of all kinds of tello for sizing and dyeing, available in 4 qualities: simple, cationic, non-ionic and cationic + non-ionic (pack 125–240 KG). It gives cost effective results with extra benefits to customers in sizing.

Eco-SilSoftner Range — Silicone Softeners

The Eco-SilSoftner range has three grades: Eco-SilSoftner Glazer (sizing, glazing and strengthening the yarn), Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophobic) with a water-repellent feel, and Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophilic) with an absorbent feel (packs 60 / 150 / 240 / 1,000 KG).

EcoFix-FB — Dye Fixer

EcoFix-FB is a formaldehyde-based fixer for enhanced wash fastness of reactive and direct dyes on cellulosic fibres, used in the dyeing stage.

EcoFlame-FR Pro — Flame Retardant Finish

EcoFlame-FR Pro is a fire proof (imported) durable flame retardant finish for cotton, blends and technical textiles.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between EcoSoftener D-11 and the Eco-SilSoftner range?
EcoSoftener D-11 is a white thick paste, a replacement of all kinds of tello for sizing and dyeing, available in 4 qualities: simple, cationic, non-ionic and cationic + non-ionic (pack 125–240 KG). The Eco-SilSoftner range has three grades: Eco-SilSoftner Glazer (sizing, glazing and strengthening the yarn), Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophobic) with a water-repellent feel, and Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophilic) with an absorbent feel (packs 60 / 150 / 240 / 1,000 KG).
What is EcoFix-FB and when is it used?
EcoFix-FB is a formaldehyde-based fixer for enhanced wash fastness of reactive and direct dyes on cellulosic fibres, used in the dyeing stage. Ask Seamens for dosing and pack sizes for your process.
What is EcoFlame-FR Pro used for?
EcoFlame-FR Pro is a fire proof (imported) durable flame retardant finish for cotton, blends and technical textiles. Contact Seamens for application guidance and trial support.
Can I use EcoSoftener D-11 and EcoFix-FB in the same bath?
Do not assume so. Fixers and softeners differ in ionic character, so run a compatibility trial first. Seamens technical team can advise the correct sequence for your dyeing and finishing process.
Are these products made in Germany?
Yes. The ECO-Series is German based organic chemical technology, made in Germany, supplied in Pakistan by Seamens Enterprises, Lahore.
