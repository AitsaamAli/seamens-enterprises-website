Warp Sizing Chemicals for Textile Mills Pakistan — ECO-Series

The ECO-Series warp sizing range for Pakistan's weaving mills: EcoSize-CXW-111, EcoShort-GS-777, EcoAcryllic-ACL-55 and EcoSoftener D-11.

June 20263 min read
Key Takeaways
  • EcoSize-CXW-111 — PVA saver sizing powder; replaces PVAs and powder chemicals, no starch or wax to add
  • EcoShort-GS-777 — multi-polymers film forming sizing agent: size + lubricant + binder
  • EcoAcryllic-ACL-55 — modified polymer sizing agent for denim, towel, canvas and low count cotton
  • EcoSoftener D-11 — replacement of tello for sizing and dyeing, in 4 qualities
  • All are German based organic chemical technology, made in Germany

Warp sizing is among the most technically critical steps in weaving. A well-sized warp runs with fewer breakages and steadier loom efficiency; a poorly sized warp causes stoppages and quality rejects. Seamens Enterprises supplies the ECO-Series sizing range for Pakistan's weaving mills.

EcoSize-CXW-111 — PVA Saver Sizing Powder

White granular powder, 25 KG bag or 100 KG plastic drum. It combines PVA (all kinds) saver polymers, body enhancer, friction reducer, penetration booster, static control and an anti-foam system. It is a replacement of all kinds of PVAs and powder chemicals; starch, wax and polymers are already included, so they need not be added to the size mix. See the EcoSize-CXW-111 comparison and dosing tables.

EcoShort-GS-777 — Multi-Polymer Sizing Agent

White thick paste, packing 125–240 KG. A multi-polymers film forming organic sizing agent, a replacement of all kinds of PVA powders (for dyeing and sizing). Results = size + lubricant + binder. For yarn: high strength, smooth surface of looms, good adhesion, abrasion resistance. For production: easy desizing, low foam, stability, cost effective. For fabric quality: less stains, better weavability, soft hand feel.

EcoAcryllic-ACL-55 — For Denim, Towel and Canvas

Transparent thick liquid, packs 60 / 150 / 240 / 1,000 KG. A modified polymer based high performance sizing agent used for denim, towel, canvas/drill, tent/tarpaulin, bags/sacking and low count cotton. Denim (indigo yarn): high abrasion resistance technology, low size add-on, easy desizing, penetration, no gumming. Towel (100% cotton / combed yarn): high strength, soft hand, low foam, economical compared to all PVAs.

EcoSoftener D-11 — Replacement of Tello

White thick paste, packing 125–240 KG. A replacement of all kinds of tello (and wax) for sizing and dyeing, available in 4 qualities: simple, cationic, non-ionic, and cationic + non-ionic.

Desizing

After weaving, the size is removed in desizing. EcoDesize-EZ is an enzymatic desizing agent for fast, effective removal of starch and synthetic sizes from woven fabrics.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is warp sizing and why does it matter for weaving?
Warp sizing applies a protective film to warp yarns before weaving. It strengthens the yarn and improves abrasion resistance so it withstands loom stress, which cuts breakages and stoppages.
What is EcoSize-CXW-111?
EcoSize-CXW-111 is a white granular sizing powder (25 KG bag or 100 KG plastic drum): German based organic technology, made in Germany, with PVA saver polymers, body enhancer, friction reducer, penetration booster, static control and anti-foam in one powder. It replaces all kinds of PVAs and powder chemicals, and no starch or wax needs to be added.
What is EcoShort-GS-777?
EcoShort-GS-777 is a white thick paste (125–240 KG pack): a multi-polymers film forming organic sizing agent, a replacement of all kinds of PVA powders for dyeing and sizing. It works as size, lubricant and binder in one.
Which product is for denim and towel yarn?
EcoAcryllic-ACL-55, a transparent thick liquid: a modified polymer based high performance sizing agent for denim, towel, canvas/drill, tent/tarpaulin, bags/sacking and low count cotton (packs 60 / 150 / 240 / 1,000 KG).
What removes the size after weaving?
Desizing is the first wet processing step. Seamens supplies EcoDesize-EZ, an enzymatic desizing agent for fast, effective removal of starch and synthetic sizes from woven fabrics.
