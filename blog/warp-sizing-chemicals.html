Warp sizing is among the most technically critical steps in textile weaving. A well-sized warp runs with minimal breakages, high loom efficiency, and consistent fabric quality. A poorly sized warp creates constant loom stoppages, quality rejects, high knotting labour, and ultimately uncompetitive fabric production costs. In Pakistan's highly competitive weaving sector — from the broad-looms of Faisalabad to the shuttle looms of Karachi — the choice of sizing agent and the precision of its application determines whether a mill runs efficiently or struggles.

Seamens Enterprises supplies ECO-Series warp sizing agents designed for Pakistan's yarn types, water conditions, and sizing machine configurations. This guide covers both key ECO-Series sizing products: EcoSize-CXW-111 and EcoPVA-GS.

EcoSize-CXW-111 — Modified Starch Sizing Agent

EcoSize-CXW-111 is Seamens Enterprises' high-performance modified starch sizing agent. Unlike raw maize or tapioca starch, EcoSize-CXW-111 is chemically modified to provide:

  • Lower viscosity at higher concentration — allows higher add-on without the paste becoming too thick to penetrate the yarn bundle
  • Stable viscosity over the sizing machine run — less variation in add-on from beam start to beam end
  • Flexible sizing film — reduced fibre shedding and improved loom shedding performance compared to unmodified starch
  • Clean desizing — removes completely with standard amylase enzymatic desizing; no residue that interferes with pre-treatment or dyeing

Suitable Applications

  • Cotton yarn Ne 6/1 to Ne 40/1 (coarse to medium-count weaving)
  • Cotton-polyester blends up to 50% polyester
  • Viscose (rayon) warp yarns
  • Denim and canvas weaving where high add-on is required
  • Can be used alone or blended with EcoPVA-GS for improved performance at medium counts

EcoPVA-GS — Polyvinyl Alcohol Sizing Agent

EcoPVA-GS is Seamens Enterprises' polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) sizing agent, providing the higher film strength and flexibility required for demanding weaving applications where starch alone is insufficient:

  • Superior abrasion resistance — PVA film is significantly stronger than starch film. Critical for high-speed airjet and rapier looms where warp yarns are subjected to intensive mechanical stress.
  • Excellent film flexibility — PVA film bends with the yarn during shed opening without cracking or flaking onto loom components
  • Low hairiness improvement — PVA binds protruding fibres to the yarn surface more effectively than starch, reducing fibre fly and loom contamination
  • Hot water desizeable — PVA is water-soluble and removed by hot water wash (70–95°C) without enzyme requirement; saves desizing chemical cost

Suitable Applications

  • Fine cotton counts Ne 40/1 and above
  • 100% polyester and polyester-cotton fine count weaving
  • Airjet and rapier loom operations at 600+ rpm
  • Blended with EcoSize-CXW-111 for medium counts (typically 20–40% PVA blend)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is warp sizing and why is it critical for weaving?
Warp sizing applies a protective coating to warp yarns before weaving. The size film strengthens fibres, reduces hairiness, and improves abrasion resistance so yarns can withstand the mechanical stress of the loom. Without adequate sizing, warp yarn breakage rates on the loom increase dramatically — causing production stoppages, quality defects, and increased labour costs. Seamens ECO-Series sizing agents EcoSize-CXW-111 and EcoPVA-GS are designed specifically for Pakistan's textile weaving sector.
What is EcoSize-CXW-111 and what yarn counts is it suitable for?
EcoSize-CXW-111 is Seamens Enterprises' modified starch-based warp sizing agent for cotton, cotton-polyester blends, and viscose warp yarns. It provides high add-on levels with good film strength and flexibility — suitable for coarse to medium counts (Ne 6/1 to Ne 40/1). It is easily biodegradable, desizes cleanly with amylase, and is compatible with PVA blending for fine count applications.
What is EcoPVA-GS and when is it used instead of starch?
EcoPVA-GS is Seamens Enterprises' polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) warp sizing agent. PVA provides superior film strength and abrasion resistance compared to starch — making it preferred for fine counts (Ne 40/1+), synthetic fibres, and high-speed loom operations. EcoPVA-GS is also used in blends with EcoSize-CXW-111 for medium counts to achieve cost-performance balance.
How do you remove warp size (desize) after weaving?
EcoSize-CXW-111 (starch-based) is removed by enzymatic desizing with amylase enzymes, or by oxidative desizing with hydrogen peroxide. EcoPVA-GS is water-soluble and removed by hot water washing (70–95°C) without enzymes. Proper desizing before bleaching and dyeing is essential — residual size causes unlevel dyeing and processing problems.
What sizing add-on percentage should I use for my yarn count?
General guidelines: coarse counts (Ne 6–20) need 8–12% add-on with EcoSize-CXW-111; medium counts (Ne 20–40) need 10–15% with a blend; fine counts (Ne 40+) need 14–18% with EcoPVA-GS dominant. Seamens Enterprises' technical team can review your sizing recipe and recommend the optimal blend ratio for your specific yarn and loom combination.
