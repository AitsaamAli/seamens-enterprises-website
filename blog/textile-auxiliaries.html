What Are Textile Auxiliary Chemicals?

Textile auxiliary chemicals are specialty formulations used at every stage of fabric processing, from raw greige to finished fabric. Seamens Enterprises has supplied textile auxiliaries to Pakistani mills since 1997. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are textile auxiliary chemicals?
Textile auxiliary chemicals are specialty chemicals used at every stage of fabric processing: sizing, pre-treatment, dyeing, finishing and specialty finishes.
Q: How many ECO-Series products does Seamens supply?
The ECO-Series has 19 products covering sizing, pre-treatment, dyeing, softening and finishing, and specialty finishes. All are German based organic chemical technology, made in Germany.
Q: Which sizing products are available?
EcoSize-CXW-111 (PVA saver sizing powder, 25 KG bag), EcoShort-GS-777 (multi-polymers sizing agent paste), EcoAcryllic-ACL-55 (modified polymer sizing agent for denim and towel), EcoPVA-GS (PVA-based sizing glue) and the Eco-SilSoftner Glazer for glazing and strengthening yarn.
Q: What is the difference between EcoSoftener D-11 and the Eco-SilSoftner range?
EcoSoftener D-11 is a replacement of tello for sizing and dyeing, available in 4 qualities (simple, cationic, non-ionic, cationic + non-ionic). The Eco-SilSoftner range are silicone softeners: Glazer for sizing and glazing, Hydrophobic and Hydrophilic for the finished feel.

Yes. Seamens Enterprises offers confidential, no-obligation product trials at your mill. Contact the team on WhatsApp or through the contact form.
