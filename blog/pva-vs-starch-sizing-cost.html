What Drives the Cost of EcoSize-CXW-111 Per Beam?

Cost per beam is driven mainly by dosing rate, and dosing rate is driven by yarn count — finer counts generally need a bit more chemical per kilogram of yarn. EcoSize-CXW-111 is a one-bag, ready-to-use sizing powder, so there's no separate starch, wax, or polymer purchase to add on top of the dosing figure.

Dosing Varies By Yarn Count

Dosing is confirmed by a short trial on your sizing machine for each specific construction — as a general guide, coarser counts need a lower dose per 100 kg of yarn and finer counts need somewhat more, since finer yarn has more surface area and less inherent strength. Seamens provides the exact working figure for your construction directly, rather than a one-size-fits-all number.

Why the One-Bag Formulation Saves Money

A conventional sizing recipe usually means separately sourcing and cooking several additives — each with its own cost, its own mixing step, and its own gas consumption to bring to temperature. EcoSize-CXW-111 replaces that whole stack with one ready-to-use powder, which is where most of the real cost saving shows up: not in a lower raw-chemical price alone, but in labour, gas, and mixing time removed from the sizing kitchen.

  • One mixing step — instead of several separate chemicals cooked and blended individually
  • Lower gas use — fewer components to bring to working temperature
  • Minimal size-box foam — fewer machine stoppages to clear foam
  • Reduced static — helps on PC/poly-blend counts

Some qualities and counts may still call for a small additional top-up alongside EcoSize-CXW-111 depending on the specific construction — Seamens confirms this during the trial run rather than as a standard part of the recipe.

Getting an Exact Quote for Your Beam

Because dosing scales with yarn count and beam size, an exact PKR figure needs your specific construction — Seamens provides a written dosing and cost estimate after a short trial run on your sizing machine.

Frequently Asked Questions

What drives the cost of EcoSize-CXW-111 per beam?
Cost per beam is driven mainly by dosing rate, which scales with yarn count — finer counts generally need somewhat more chemical per 100 kg of yarn than coarser counts. Because EcoSize-CXW-111 is a one-bag ready formulation, there's no separate starch, wax, or polymer purchase to add to that figure.
How is EcoSize-CXW-111 dosing determined?
Dosing is confirmed by a short trial on your sizing machine for the specific construction, since it varies by yarn count and quality. Seamens provides the exact working figure directly rather than a generic rate.
Does the one-bag formulation actually lower total sizing cost?
Yes. Because starch, wax, body-enhancer, and anti-static agents are already combined in the one bag, mills save on separate chemical purchases, mixing labour, and the gas used to cook multiple components separately — all of which add up alongside the raw chemical price.
Is EcoSize-CXW-111 suitable for every yarn count?
EcoSize-CXW-111 is formulated for Ne 6 to Ne 40 cotton yarn and covers this range well. Some specific counts or qualities may still need a small top-up of starch, wax, or polymer, which Seamens confirms during the trial run for a new construction.
