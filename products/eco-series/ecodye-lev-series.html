EcoDye-LEV Series: Levelling and Dispersing Agents for Dyeing

The EcoDye-LEV Series is a range of levelling and dispersing agents for reactive and disperse dyeing processes. Levelling helps the dye go on evenly, and dispersing keeps disperse dye particles spread through the bath instead of clumping.

Two jobs in the dye bath

In reactive dyeing, a dye that strikes too fast fixes unevenly and leaves streaks or shade differences. A levelling agent slows and evens that uptake so the whole lot reaches the same depth.

Disperse dyes for polyester are not dissolved; they are fine particles held in suspension. If they agglomerate, they leave specks and spots. A dispersing agent keeps them evenly spread through the dyeing cycle.

Choosing within the series

The right product depends on the dye class, the machine and the shade. Seamens recommends from the series after reviewing your recipe and confirms it on a trial batch.

How EcoDye-LEV Series works

Reactive dyes can rush onto cotton in the first minutes of dyeing, fixing where they land and leaving streaks. Disperse dyes for polyester are fine particles that must stay evenly spread or they form specks. Levelling and dispersing agents manage both behaviours.

The EcoDye-LEV Series provides levelling agents that slow and even dye uptake and dispersing agents that keep disperse dye particles separated through the heating cycle. The product and dose are matched to the dye class and machine.

Benefits of EcoDye-LEV Series in detail

Fewer re-dyes

Getting the shade right first time saves the water, energy, chemicals and machine time a re-dye consumes.

Better right-first-time rate

Predictable dyeing improves on-time delivery and reduces lab-to-bulk adjustments.

Suits reactive and disperse dyeing

One series covers the two main dye classes used in Pakistani mills.

Cleaner machines

Well-dispersed dye is less likely to deposit on machine parts, reducing machine cleaning between lots.

Where EcoDye-LEV Series delivers the most value

Reactive dyeing of cotton knits

Knit dye houses run many short batches. Levelling control helps keep shade even from batch to batch and reduces corrective re-dyes.

Pale and pastel shades

Light shades show every unevenness. Good levelling and dispersion help these shades come out uniform the first time.

Viscose and cellulosic blends

Viscose takes dye quickly and can streak. The series helps manage dye uptake for more even results.
