EcoWet+Det: Wetting and Detergent Action in One Product

EcoWet+Det is a combined high-performance wetting and detergent agent for thorough fabric preparation and deep scouring. It brings fast wetting and soil removal together, so one product does a job that often needs two.

Two functions, one addition

Wetting lets water enter the fabric quickly; detergency lifts oils and dirt and keeps them from settling back. In many preparation baths these come from two separate products, each dosed and stocked on its own.

EcoWet+Det combines both, which simplifies the recipe and helps the bath work evenly through the full fabric thickness.

Where it is used

It suits deep scouring and general preparation on batch and continuous equipment. The amount is set from your fabric and soiling and confirmed on a trial lot.

How EcoWet+Det works

Wetting and detergency are two halves of good preparation. A wetting agent lowers surface tension so liquor enters the fabric fast; a detergent lifts oil and dirt and keeps them suspended so they do not settle back.

EcoWet+Det combines both actions. Fewer separate additions make the recipe simpler and help each batch behave the same way.
