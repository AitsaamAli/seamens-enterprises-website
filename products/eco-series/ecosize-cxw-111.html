EcoSize-CXW-111: All-in-One Warp Sizing Powder

EcoSize-CXW-111 is a ready-to-use warp sizing powder from the ECO-Series. It replaces all kinds of PVA and powder sizing chemicals in one product, because starch, wax and polymers are already included, so the size mix needs fewer separate ingredients.

Why mills switch to one sizing powder

A conventional size kitchen often blends PVA, starch, wax and several additives, each bought, stored and weighed separately. Every extra ingredient is another chance for a weighing error that shows up later as hairiness, breaks or uneven add-on on the loom.

EcoSize-CXW-111 is built to cut that complexity. The mill mixes one powder instead of a recipe of five or six, which simplifies stock control and makes the size box more consistent from shift to shift. For some quality counts a small top-up of other items may still help, and that is decided during the trial.

Where it fits in your sizing line

The powder is cooked and applied in the size box like any conventional size. It is used on cotton warp and on polyester blends such as PC and PV constructions. Indicative doses and savings from the owner's comparison sheet are shown in the technical tables below, and every recommendation is confirmed on your own sizing machine.

How EcoSize-CXW-111 works

Sizing puts a thin, flexible coating on each warp end so it survives the rubbing of heald eyes, drop wires and reed dents. The coating must stick to the fibre, bend without cracking and leave the yarn surface smooth enough that loose fibres lie flat instead of tangling with neighbouring ends.

EcoSize-CXW-111 delivers that coating from one cooked powder. Because the components a mill would normally weigh separately are already balanced inside the product, every batch in the size kitchen starts from the same recipe, and the size box behaves the same way on the morning shift as on the night shift.

Benefits of EcoSize-CXW-111 in detail

Lower total sizing cost

Buying, storing and weighing one product instead of PVA, starch, wax and additives cuts purchasing effort, warehouse space and size-kitchen labour. The owner comparison sheet below shows the dosing and savings Seamens records against a common PVA recipe.

Fewer warp breaks on the loom

A well-bonded, flexible film resists abrasion, so ends break less often. Fewer breaks mean fewer stoppages, higher loom efficiency and less knotting work for weavers on every shift.

Consistent size from batch to batch

A pre-balanced powder removes the weighing errors that come with multi-ingredient recipes. Consistent add-on gives predictable weaving and fewer shade problems after desizing and dyeing.

Good adhesion on blends

The size adheres well on polyester-cotton and polyester-viscose yarns as well as cotton, so one product can cover more of the mill's constructions.

Low foam in the size box

Low foaming keeps the size level stable and the pick-up even across the warp sheet, which protects quality on wide beams.

Simpler stock control

One code in the store instead of five or six makes reordering, FIFO rotation and audits straightforward for the stores and purchase teams.

Where EcoSize-CXW-111 delivers the most value

Integrated mill with a mixed loom fleet

A mill running older rapier looms beside new air-jet machines needs one size that protects warp on both. EcoSize-CXW-111 gives a single, pre-balanced recipe, so the sizing master is not juggling separate starch, binder and lubricant additions for each loom group.

Commission sizing unit

Small sizing units serve many weavers with different yarns. A powder that arrives ready to cook in 25 KG bags reduces weighing errors and keeps beams consistent from one customer order to the next, which protects the unit's reputation with its weaving clients.

Export sheeting with strict defect limits

Buyers of bedsheet and shirting fabric count every warp-related fault. Fewer breaks mean fewer knots, starts and stops, so more fabric meets the first-quality grade and less is sold as seconds.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Pakistani weaving mills run long shifts on mixed fleets of older shuttleless and newer air-jet looms, often with cotton and polyester-cotton yarn from several spinners. Recipes that worked on one yarn lot can behave differently on the next. A single, pre-balanced sizing powder reduces one source of variation in that environment, and the 25 KG bag or 100 KG drum suits both small sizing departments and large integrated mills in Faisalabad and Lahore.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Run the trial on one construction first, ideally the one with the highest break rate, so the improvement is easy to see.
  • Record warp breaks per 100,000 picks for a week before the trial to have a fair baseline.
  • Keep size box temperature and viscosity logs during the trial; consistent cooking is as important as the product.
  • Check desizing on the first fabric lot so preparation is confirmed alongside weaving.
  • Once results are proven, move other constructions one at a time rather than all at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EcoSize-CXW-111 used for?
It is a warp sizing agent. It is applied to warp yarn before weaving to give the yarn strength and abrasion resistance, and it replaces separate PVA and powder sizing chemicals.
Do I still need to add starch or wax with EcoSize-CXW-111?
In most recipes no, because starch, wax and polymers are already included in the powder. For some quality counts a small addition may help; this is confirmed during the trial.
What pack sizes does EcoSize-CXW-111 come in?
EcoSize-CXW-111 is supplied as a white granular powder in a 25 KG bag or a 100 KG plastic drum.
Is EcoSize-CXW-111 suitable for polyester blends?
Yes. It is used on PC 80/20 and PV 65/35 type blends as well as 100% cotton, with doses confirmed by trial.
How quickly can a mill switch to EcoSize-CXW-111?
Most mills run a trial beam first. Once loom results are confirmed, the recipe is switched for that construction and extended to others.
Does EcoSize-CXW-111 affect desizing?
The size is designed to come off in normal desizing. Desizing results are checked during the trial so preparation is not affected.
Can EcoSize-CXW-111 help with high break rates in summer?
Hot, dry conditions raise breaks in many halls. A good size film helps, and humidity control in the weaving hall matters too.
Who supports the trial?
The Seamens technical team supports the trial, from recipe set-up to reviewing loom results.
