EcoFix-FB: Dye Fixer for Better Wash Fastness

EcoFix-FB is a formaldehyde-based fixing agent that enhances the wash fastness of reactive and direct dyes on cellulosic fibres such as cotton and viscose. It is applied after dyeing to reduce colour bleeding in washing.

Why an after-fix is used

Some dye, especially on deep shades and with direct dyes, is held loosely and can bleed in the first washes, staining white parts or other garments. A fixer is applied after dyeing to lock more of that colour in place and improve wash fastness.

Check compliance before choosing

EcoFix-FB is formaldehyde-based. Some buyers and brands restrict formaldehyde in finished goods. Confirm your buyer's chemical requirements with Seamens before selecting a fixer, and ask for current compliance documents for your order.

How EcoFix-FB works

After dyeing, some dye sits on the fibre without being firmly fixed. In washing it bleeds out and stains other parts of the garment or other items in the wash. A fixing agent forms a complex with this loosely held dye so it stays on the fibre.

EcoFix-FB is a formaldehyde-based fixer for reactive and direct dyes on cellulosic fibres, applied as an after-treatment.

Buyer's guide to EcoFix-FB

What a fixing agent does

Direct and some reactive dyes can bleed in washing. A cationic fixing agent forms a complex with dye on the fibre, reducing bleeding and improving wet fastness.

Formaldehyde-based chemistry

EcoFix-FB is formaldehyde-based. Many buyers set limits for free formaldehyde in finished textiles, so check the buyer's restricted substance list before use.

Testing fastness

Wash fastness is tested to ISO 105-C06.

Shade change

Some shade change can occur and is checked in the trial.

Buyer compliance

Checking RSL limits protects orders.

Wet fastness

Better wet fastness reduces complaints.

When to choose another fixer

If a buyer's restricted substance list sets very low formaldehyde limits, a formaldehyde-free fixing approach may be needed. Seamens can discuss options with you for those orders, while EcoFix-FB remains a practical choice for orders where it complies.

What a successful result looks like

Improved wash fastness ratings, acceptable shade change and compliance with the buyer's formaldehyde limit together confirm that EcoFix-FB is the right fixer for the order. These results are documented for each order.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Pakistani dyers serving many buyers face different chemical restrictions per brand. Knowing each buyer's formaldehyde limits is essential before choosing a fixer.
