EcoStab-HS: Peroxide Stabilizer for Controlled Bleaching

EcoStab-HS is a hydrogen peroxide stabilizer for controlled, uniform bleaching. It helps keep peroxide working at a steady rate, which supports even whiteness and helps prevent fabric damage during scouring and pre-treatment.

Why peroxide needs a stabilizer

Hydrogen peroxide bleaches cotton by releasing active oxygen. If it decomposes too quickly, for example because of metal traces in the water or fabric, the bleaching is uneven and the fibre can be attacked, causing pinholes and strength loss.

A stabilizer controls that release so the peroxide works steadily through the whole bleaching time, giving an even white without damaging the fabric.

In practice

EcoStab-HS is added to the peroxide bleach bath. Water quality has a big effect on bleaching, so Seamens looks at your water and recipe before recommending the amount and confirms it on a trial lot.

How EcoStab-HS works

Peroxide bleaches by releasing active oxygen. Traces of iron, copper and hardness in water and fabric speed this up uncontrollably, wasting peroxide and attacking the cotton, which shows as pinholes and lost strength.

EcoStab-HS controls that decomposition so active oxygen is released steadily through the bleaching time. The result is more even whiteness, better use of peroxide and protection of the fibre.

Where EcoStab-HS delivers the most value

Hard or variable process water

Water with high hardness or metal content can decompose peroxide unevenly. A stabiliser keeps bleaching controlled even when water changes.

Combined scour and bleach

Single-bath scour-bleach saves time but stresses the bath. Stabilising peroxide protects fabric strength while reaching whiteness.

High-white fabrics for printing

Print bases need high, even whiteness. Controlled bleaching gives a reliable base for bright prints.

What a successful result looks like

Success shows as even whiteness across the batch, no pinholes or weak spots, steady fabric strength and lower variation between batches when water quality changes. Seamens reviews whiteness readings and strength results with your preparation team before the stabiliser is adopted for bulk production.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Groundwater around Lahore and Faisalabad varies in hardness and metal content. That variation is a common cause of uneven peroxide bleaching, which a stabiliser helps manage.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Have your process water tested before setting the dose.
  • Check whiteness and strength together on trial lots.
  • Keep peroxide dosing accurate; stabiliser does not replace correct dosing.
  • Inspect fabric for pinholes after bleaching.
  • Record bath temperature and time for repeatability.
