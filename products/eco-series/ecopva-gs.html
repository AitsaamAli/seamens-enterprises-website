EcoPVA-GS: PVA Sizing Glue for Strong, Flexible Yarn Coating

EcoPVA-GS is a polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) based sizing glue for strong, flexible yarn coating with excellent film-forming adhesion. It is suited to fine counts and synthetic yarns, where a tough, elastic film matters most.

Why PVA for fine and synthetic yarns

Fine counts and synthetic filaments have smooth, low-hairiness surfaces that ordinary starch does not grip well. PVA forms a strong, elastic film that adheres to these surfaces and flexes with the yarn on the loom.

EcoPVA-GS supplies that film-forming adhesion as a ready sizing glue for mills that work with these yarns.

Selecting your sizing product

Many mills use more than one sizing product across their constructions. Seamens helps decide where EcoPVA-GS fits against the rest of the ECO-Series sizing range and confirms the choice by trial.

How EcoPVA-GS works

PVA forms one of the strongest, most elastic films used in sizing. It grips smooth synthetic and fine yarns that starch alone cannot hold, and it stretches with the yarn rather than cracking.

EcoPVA-GS is a PVA-based sizing glue that gives strong, flexible coating with film-forming adhesion. It is used where yarn surfaces are smooth and loom demands are high.

Desizing

Recipe balance

Recipes are balanced for each construction.

Adhesion to synthetics

Polyester and other synthetic fibres have smooth surfaces where starch adheres poorly. PVA bonds well and forms a flexible film.

Lower breaks on fine counts

Consistent cooking

Consistent cooking and viscosity keep add-on steady.

Trial plan

Trial on one fine-count or synthetic construction first, measure yarn strength, abrasion and loom breaks, then extend.

What a successful result looks like

A good outcome is fewer warp breaks per 100,000 picks on the trial construction, stable yarn strength after sizing, little shedding at the dropper and heald area, and complete removal in desizing. When all four are confirmed on bulk beams, the recipe can be rolled out to similar fine-count and synthetic qualities with confidence.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

As Pakistani mills move into finer counts and synthetic blends for higher-value exports, sizing needs change. A PVA sizing glue supports these constructions.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Use on fine counts and synthetics where adhesion matters most.
  • Measure yarn strength before and after sizing.
  • Confirm desizing on the first fabric lot.
  • Combine with other sizes only as advised in the trial.
  • Keep cooking conditions consistent.
