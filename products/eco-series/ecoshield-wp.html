EcoShield-WP: Durable Water Repellent Finish for Outdoor Fabrics

EcoShield-WP is a durable water repellent (DWR) finish for technical and outdoor fabrics. It makes water bead up and roll off the surface instead of soaking in, while the fabric stays breathable.

Repellent, not waterproof

A water repellent finish changes the fabric surface so water forms beads and runs off. It is different from a waterproof coating or membrane, which blocks water completely; a DWR finish keeps the fabric breathable.

Typical uses are outerwear shells, tents, bags and covers, where light rain and splashes should not soak the fabric.

Specification first

Buyers usually set a spray rating or repellency test. Share it with Seamens before the trial so the finish and curing conditions are matched to that test.

How EcoShield-WP works

A durable water repellent finish lowers the surface energy of the fibre so water forms round beads instead of spreading and soaking in. The weave stays open, so air and vapour still pass through.

EcoShield-WP is applied in finishing and cured to fix it on the fabric. The curing conditions and dose are set against the repellency rating your buyer specifies.

Preparing fabric for repellent finishing

Water repellent finishes are very sensitive to residues. Wetting agents, softeners and detergents left from earlier processes can attract water and spoil the spray rating. For best results, fabric should be well washed and neutral before finishing, and the finishing bath should be free of incompatible auxiliaries. Seamens reviews the full process route during the trial so the finish performs as intended on the first bulk lot.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EcoShield-WP?
A durable water repellent (DWR) finish for technical and outdoor fabrics.
Is a DWR finish the same as waterproofing?
No. A DWR finish makes water bead and roll off while the fabric stays breathable; waterproofing blocks water completely.
What pack sizes are available?
60, 150, 240 and 1,000 KG packs.
What spray rating can EcoShield-WP achieve?
Results depend on fabric and process; the target rating is agreed with the buyer and confirmed in the trial.
Is the finish affected by washing?
Durability is tested after the washing cycles the buyer specifies.
Does it make fabric waterproof?
No. It makes fabric water repellent and breathable, not waterproof.
Can residual wetting agents affect repellency?
Yes. Residues from preparation can reduce repellency, so fabric must be clean.
Is curing required?
Yes, the finish is cured to fix it.
