Matching the softener to the end use

Silicone softeners give the smooth, slippery handle buyers associate with premium fabric. A hydrophobic grade adds a water-repellent character to that feel, which suits outerwear, bottom-weights and fabrics that are not expected to absorb moisture.

Where absorbency matters, such as towels, the hydrophilic grade is the better choice.

In the finishing range

It is applied in finishing on standard padding equipment. The amount is set from the target handle and confirmed on a trial lot.

How Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophobic) works

Silicone softeners spread over fibre surfaces and lower friction between fibres, which the hand reads as smoothness. The hydrophobic grade orients its silicone so the surface also resists water.

Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophobic) is applied in finishing to give that silky, water-repellent feel on fabrics where absorbency is not required.

Buyer's guide to Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophobic)

A premium silky hand

Hydrophobic silicone softeners give a smooth, slick and luxurious hand that buyers notice immediately. They are suited to apparel fabrics where water absorbency is not a requirement, such as trousers, jackets and fashion fabrics.

Where not to use it

Because it reduces water absorbency, this grade is not used on towels, bath products or goods that must absorb moisture. For those, the hydrophilic grade is used.

Combining with other finishes

Softener selection is considered alongside any resin, repellent or other finish so the finishes work together.

Durability

Handle after repeated washing is assessed during the trial.

Consistent handle lot to lot

A stable finishing recipe helps every lot match the approved sample.

Value-added finishing

A premium hand helps fabrics command better prices.

Buyer approval

Matching the approved sample builds buyer trust.

Running a fair trial

Finish a trial lot alongside your current softener and let the buyer's merchandiser compare handles blind. A side-by-side comparison on the same fabric is the clearest way to judge the improvement.

What a successful result looks like

Success means a silky, smooth handle that matches or improves on the buyer's reference, no change in shade or whiteness, and a handle that remains acceptable after the washes the buyer specifies. Once confirmed on bulk, the recipe is recorded so every repeat order feels the same.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophobic)?
A silicone finishing softener with a hydrophobic, water-repellent feel.
When should I choose the hydrophilic grade instead?
When the fabric must stay absorbent, for example towels; the hydrophilic grade keeps an absorbent feel.
What pack sizes are available?
60, 150, 240 and 1,000 KG packs.
Is this grade suitable for towels?
No. Towels need absorbency; use the Hydrophilic grade.
Does it make fabric waterproof?
No. It gives a hydrophobic feel; a DWR finish such as EcoShield-WP is used for water repellency.
Which fabrics suit it?
Outerwear, bottom-weights and fabrics finished for handle rather than absorbency.
Is it used on denim?
Fabric suitability is confirmed in the trial.
Will it yellow white fabric?
Shade and whiteness are checked in the trial.
