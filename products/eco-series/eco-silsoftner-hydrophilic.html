Softness without losing absorbency

Many softeners make fabric feel soft but reduce how fast it absorbs water. For a towel that is a real fault: it feels good in the shop and then fails at its main job.

A hydrophilic silicone softener is designed to give the soft handle while keeping the fabric absorbent, so the product works as well as it feels.

Typical uses

Towels, bathrobes, bedding and other home textiles are the main applications. The amount is set from the handle and absorbency target and confirmed on a trial lot.

How Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophilic) works

Ordinary silicone softeners can coat fibres with a water-repellent layer. A hydrophilic silicone is modified so the softened surface still attracts water, letting the fabric wick and absorb.

Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophilic) gives the soft silicone handle while keeping an absorbent feel, the combination towels and bedding need.

Buyer's guide to Eco-SilSoftner (Hydrophilic)

Softness without losing absorbency

Conventional silicone softeners give an excellent hand but can make fabric repel water, which is a serious fault on towels and bath products. A hydrophilic silicone grade is built to give a soft, smooth feel while allowing water to wick into the fabric, so towels still dry the skin and bedding stays breathable.

How absorbency is tested

Absorbency is checked with recognised methods such as drop absorbency or wicking tests. Running these tests beside handle evaluation during the trial shows that the finish meets both requirements.

Durability through washing

Home textiles are washed many times. Testing handle and absorbency after repeated home laundering shows how the finish performs in use.

Choosing between the three Eco-SilSoftner grades

Glazer is for yarn and weaving, Hydrophobic for a silky hand where absorbency is not required, and Hydrophilic for absorbent goods. Seamens helps match the grade to the end use.

Shade and whiteness

Whiteness and shade are checked so the softener does not change the colour buyers approved.

Towel exports

Soft, absorbent towels support export orders.

What a successful result looks like

A soft, smooth handle, fast absorbency and unchanged whiteness, confirmed after washing, show the finish meets towel and bedding requirements.
