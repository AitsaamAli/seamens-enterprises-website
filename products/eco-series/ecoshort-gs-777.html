EcoShort-GS-777: Size, Lubricant and Binder in One Paste

EcoShort-GS-777 is a multi-polymer, film forming organic sizing agent supplied as a white thick paste. It works as size, lubricant and binder in a single product and is used as a replacement for all kinds of PVA powders in sizing and dyeing.

What a film forming size does for the warp

During weaving, every warp end is rubbed by heald eyes, reed wires and neighbouring yarns thousands of times. A good size forms a continuous, flexible film around the yarn that lays down protruding fibres and resists that abrasion without becoming brittle.

EcoShort-GS-777 is designed around that film. Because it also brings lubrication and binding in the same paste, the mill does not need to balance three separate products to get a smooth, strong and well-bound yarn surface.

Using it in production

The paste is added to the size mix in place of PVA powder. Results are judged on the loom: breakage rate, shedding, and how cleanly the size comes off in desizing. These are measured in a trial on your own warp; contact Seamens for trial terms.

How EcoShort-GS-777 works

A sizing film has three jobs: hold fibres to the yarn, protect it from abrasion and let it slide through the loom without friction. Mills usually reach that balance with a size plus separate lubricants and binders, adjusting each when weaving gets difficult.

EcoShort-GS-777 is a multi-polymer paste in which film forming, lubrication and binding work together. The paste goes into the size mix in place of PVA powder, and because the three functions arrive in fixed proportion, the mill adjusts one dose rather than three.

Benefits of EcoShort-GS-777 in detail

High yarn strength

The film reinforces the yarn so it withstands loom tension and abrasion, which supports higher loom speeds with fewer breaks.

Smooth yarn surface

Built-in lubrication lowers friction against heald eyes and reeds, reducing fibre shedding and fly in the weaving hall.

Good adhesion and binding

Strong binding holds protruding fibres in place, cutting hairiness and the entanglement that causes ends to stick together at the shed.

Easy desizing

The size is designed to come off cleanly in desizing, so preparation and dyeing start from an even base without residual size patches.

Low foam and good stability

Stable, low-foam behaviour keeps pick-up even across the warp sheet and makes the size box easier to run.

Fewer stains and better fabric quality

Clean running and even desizing mean fewer size stains, better weavability and a soft hand on the finished fabric.

Where EcoShort-GS-777 delivers the most value

Replacing imported PVA powder

Purchase teams buying PVA powder face price swings with the exchange rate. Moving part or all of that requirement to EcoShort-GS-777 paste simplifies buying and can reduce the number of separate size kitchen additions.

Fine-count cotton and blended warps

Finer yarns need good binding without a stiff, brittle film. The paste combines binding with lubrication, so yarn passes the reed and heald frames with less abrasion and less fly.

Multi-shift size kitchens

When three shifts share one kitchen, fewer components mean fewer chances to get a recipe wrong. One paste in 125–240 KG packs is easy to dose and record on every shift.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Many Pakistani mills still rely on imported PVA powders whose price moves with the rupee and freight costs. A paste that replaces PVA powder while also lubricating and binding gives purchase teams a simpler line item and size kitchens fewer separate additions, which matters when several shifts share one kitchen.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Replace PVA powder step by step on one beam before switching the whole recipe.
  • Watch shed opening and fly at the loom; better lubrication shows there first.
  • Confirm desizing with an iodine test on the first lot.
  • Store the paste covered and out of direct sun to keep it consistent.
  • Use the poster and product sheet to brief size kitchen staff on the new recipe.
