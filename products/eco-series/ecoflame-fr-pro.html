Where flame retardancy is required

Flame retardant performance is usually demanded by a buyer specification or a regulation for the end use, for example protective workwear, curtains and upholstery in public buildings, or industrial covers. The fabric is then tested against that standard.

A durable finish is meant to keep its performance through the washing the product will see in use, which is what separates it from a temporary treatment.

Getting it right first time

The required standard, fibre content and fabric weight all affect the application. Share the specification with Seamens before the trial so the finish and process are matched to the test the fabric must pass.
