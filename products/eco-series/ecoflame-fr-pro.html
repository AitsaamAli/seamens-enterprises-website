EcoFlame-FR Pro: Durable Flame Retardant Finish for Textiles

EcoFlame-FR Pro is an imported, durable flame retardant finish for cotton, blends and technical textiles. It is used where a fabric must resist ignition and slow flame spread, such as workwear, furnishings and industrial fabrics.

Where flame retardancy is required

Flame retardant performance is usually demanded by a buyer specification or a regulation for the end use, for example protective workwear, curtains and upholstery in public buildings, or industrial covers. The fabric is then tested against that standard.

A durable finish is meant to keep its performance through the washing the product will see in use, which is what separates it from a temporary treatment.

Getting it right first time

The required standard, fibre content and fabric weight all affect the application. Share the specification with Seamens before the trial so the finish and process are matched to the test the fabric must pass.

How EcoFlame-FR Pro works

A flame retardant finish changes how a fabric behaves when it meets a flame: it resists ignition, burns more slowly and tends to self-extinguish once the flame is removed, so a small fire is less likely to spread.

EcoFlame-FR Pro is an imported, durable finish for cotton, blends and technical textiles. Durability means the protection is designed to remain through the cleaning the product will see in use, which is what end-use standards test.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Send the buyer's flame standard before any trial.
  • Test after the required number of washes, not just after finishing.
  • Record curing temperature and time for repeatability.
  • Check handle and strength alongside flame results.
  • Keep test certificates for each lot shipped.
