Textile Auxiliary Chemicals in Lahore: The ECO-Series Range

Seventeen textile auxiliaries for sizing, pre-treatment, dyeing, softening and specialty finishing, formulated and manufactured in Pakistan.

Seamens Enterprises, LahoreUpdated October 2026
At a glance
  • 17 ECO-Series products across sizing, pre-treatment, dyeing, softening and finishing
  • Formulated and manufactured in Pakistan using imported premium raw materials; most formulations are organic-based
  • Free trial samples, evaluated on your own machine
  • Supplier since 1997 from Lahore, ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001 certified

The ECO-Series is the textile auxiliary range of Seamens Enterprises, a Lahore specialist industrial solutions company established in 1997. It covers the main wet-processing and preparation stages used by Pakistani mills: warp sizing, pre-treatment, dyeing, softening and specialty finishing.

What is the ECO-Series? German Based Organic Chemicals Technologies for Textile Auxiliaries

The ECO-Series is a 17-product range of textile auxiliary chemicals formulated and manufactured by Seamens Enterprises in Pakistan, using imported premium raw materials; most of its formulations are organic-based. Products are matched to your fabric and process through a trial before bulk supply.
