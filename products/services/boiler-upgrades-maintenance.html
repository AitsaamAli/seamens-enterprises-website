Why annual overhauls pay back

A boiler that runs all year slowly loses efficiency: tubes scale or corrode, burners drift out of tune and refractory wears. An annual overhaul catches these before they cause a breakdown or a safety risk.

Tube inspection finds thinning and damage early, and replacement is planned rather than forced by a failure.

Burner tuning and efficiency audits

A burner running with the wrong air-fuel ratio wastes fuel and produces more emissions. Tuning brings it back to an efficient setting. A thermal efficiency audit puts numbers on the losses so you can see what each improvement is worth.

How Boiler Upgrades & Maintenance works

Boiler efficiency drops gradually through scale, soot, burner drift and worn refractory. Planned maintenance restores it in stages: inspection, cleaning, repair, burner tuning and an efficiency check.

A thermal efficiency audit measures flue gas temperature and composition, blowdown losses and radiation losses to show where fuel is wasted and which fix pays back first.

Why older boilers lose efficiency

Over years of service, boilers develop soot on firesides, scale on watersides, leaking refractory and burners that drift out of adjustment. Each fault wastes a little fuel, and together they can add a significant amount to the fuel bill. An overhaul with tuning tackles all of them at once.

Flue gas analysis

Measuring oxygen, carbon monoxide and stack temperature shows how well the burner is set. Adjusting air-fuel ratio to the right range saves fuel without making combustion unsafe.

Safety devices

Overhauls include checks of safety valves, low-water cut-outs and controls.

Planning around production

Overhauls are scheduled for planned stops so steam supply is not disrupted.

What you receive after an overhaul

After each overhaul you receive a written report covering waterside and fireside condition, tube findings, burner settings, flue gas readings, safety device checks and recommended next actions, giving management a clear record of the boiler's health and the work planned for the next shutdown.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Many boilers in Pakistani factories are decades old and run long hours. A disciplined maintenance and tuning programme keeps them safe and efficient.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Schedule the annual overhaul during a planned shutdown.
  • Keep records of every inspection.
  • Tune burners after any fuel change.
  • Measure flue gas for efficiency.
  • Replace failing tubes before they leak.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a boiler overhaul include?
Annual overhauling, tube inspection and replacement where needed, and burner maintenance and tuning.
What is a thermal efficiency audit?
A survey that measures where a boiler loses heat and fuel, so improvements can be prioritised.
Can maintenance be on contract?
Yes. Planned maintenance can be arranged as a contract; see mechanical maintenance contracts.
How often should a boiler be overhauled?
Annually is common; frequency depends on hours and condition.
What does burner tuning involve?
Adjusting air-fuel ratio using flue gas measurements.
Can Seamens maintain boilers it did not supply?
Maintenance is offered for existing boilers; scope is confirmed after inspection.
What does a boiler efficiency audit from Seamens include?
It includes flue gas analysis, a review of stack temperature, blow-down, insulation and condensate losses, and a written list of recommended savings.
Can an existing boiler burner be converted to another fuel?
Fuel conversion, for example between gas and oil, is assessed case by case after reviewing the boiler, burner and fuel supply.
