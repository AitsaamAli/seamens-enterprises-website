Why automate dosing

Manual dosing depends on someone remembering, measuring and adding the right amount every shift. Under- and over-dosing both cost money: one lets scale and corrosion through, the other wastes chemical.

Automatic dosing pumps feed chemical steadily, and online TDS and pH readings show at once when water drifts out of range.

Connected to your plant

SCADA-compatible controllers let water readings and dosing appear in the plant's central system, so operators see water treatment alongside the rest of the process.

How Monitoring & Controls works

A dosing pump delivers chemical in proportion to flow or on a timer; online sensors measure TDS (conductivity) and pH continuously; a controller compares readings to set points and adjusts dosing or blowdown automatically.

SCADA-compatible controllers pass these readings to the plant control room, so water treatment is visible and alarmed alongside the rest of the process.

Buyer's guide to Monitoring & Controls

Why automatic dosing beats manual dosing

Manual dosing depends on each operator on each shift. Doses are missed, doubled or guessed. Automatic dosing pumps, linked to flow or a controller, add chemical steadily so water treatment stays within its target range around the clock.

Online TDS and pH

Online sensors measure boiler and cooling water continuously. A controller opens blow-down when TDS rises and adjusts dosing when pH drifts, saving water, chemical and fuel compared with fixed timers.

Alarms that protect equipment

Alarm limits warn operators when water quality moves out of range, so action is taken before scale or corrosion begins.

SCADA connectivity

Controllers can connect to plant SCADA, so engineers see water treatment data alongside production data and keep historic records.

Maintenance of sensors

Sensors need cleaning and calibration. Seamens sets a simple calibration routine so readings stay accurate.

Fits existing plants

Dosing pumps and controllers can be retrofitted to existing boilers and towers without major work.

Saving blow-down water

TDS-controlled blow-down discharges only when needed.

Shift consistency

Automation removes variation between shifts.

Data for audits

Logged data supports energy and water audits.

A practical upgrade path

Many plants start with a simple step, such as a dosing pump and a conductivity controller on the boiler or cooling tower, and add online pH, flow-proportional dosing and SCADA links later. Each step pays for itself through steadier treatment, less wasted blow-down and fewer manual tests, so the system can grow with your budget.
