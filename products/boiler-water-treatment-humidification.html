What does Water Safe Boiler Treatment do?

Water Safe Boiler Treatment provides oxygen scavenging, scale inhibition, pH conditioning and sludge dispersal for fire-tube and water-tube boilers, and is used to maintain alkalinity in the 10.5 to 11.5 pH range for protection. It is formulated and manufactured in Pakistan using premium organic raw materials sourced from Germany.

What humidification support does Seamens provide?

Seamens supports complete humidity control for textile spinning and weaving halls, where holding the right relative humidity reduces yarn breakage and static build-up. The range includes high-pressure fogging systems, ultrasonic humidifiers, evaporative cooling pads, and humidity sensors and control panels.

Seamens Enterprises' water treatment chemicals are certified by CONAIR and Luwa-Nederman for use in their industrial air-handling, humidification, and climate-control equipment installed across Pakistan. See our textile relative humidity chart.
