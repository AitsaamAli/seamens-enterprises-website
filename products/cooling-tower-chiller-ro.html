How is an RO antiscalant dose calculated?

Multiply the target dose in ppm by the feed flow in m3/h to get grams per hour. A 4 ppm dose on a 10 m3/h feed is 40 g/h, or 960 g per day. Confirm the dose against your feed analysis and the product guidance, and try our RO antiscalant dosing calculator.
