How do you choose the right cleaner?

Match the chemistry to the soil and the surface: oils need a degreaser, mineral scale needs a descaler, process residues need a CIP cleaner, and germs need a disinfectant. Compare price as cost per litre of ready-to-use solution, not per litre of concentrate. See our cleaning chemical supplier guide and dilution ratio chart.
