Why heavy-duty degreasing matters

Oil and grease on machinery hide leaks and wear, attract dust and make inspection harder. On floors they are a slip hazard. Engine and gearbox parts need to be clean before inspection and reassembly.

A heavy-duty degreaser breaks down that soiling so it can be wiped or rinsed away quickly.

Using it correctly

Dilution and contact time depend on how heavy the soiling is and what surface is being cleaned. Seamens advises on dilution for each job so the product works without wasting chemical or damaging sensitive surfaces.
