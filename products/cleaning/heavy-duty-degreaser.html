Why heavy-duty degreasing matters

Oil and grease on machinery hide leaks and wear, attract dust and make inspection harder. On floors they are a slip hazard. Engine and gearbox parts need to be clean before inspection and reassembly.

A heavy-duty degreaser breaks down that soiling so it can be wiped or rinsed away quickly.

Using it correctly

Dilution and contact time depend on how heavy the soiling is and what surface is being cleaned. Seamens advises on dilution for each job so the product works without wasting chemical or damaging sensitive surfaces.

How Heavy Duty Degreaser works

Grease and oil are held on surfaces by their own stickiness. A degreaser combines solvents and surfactants that penetrate, soften and lift the soil, then hold it so it rinses away.

Heavy Duty Degreaser is built for the thick, aged oil and grease found on machinery, workshop floors and engine parts, and can be applied as a liquid or spray.

Why a heavy-duty grade

Generator rooms, compressor houses, engine bays and workshop floors collect carbonised oil and baked-on grease that general cleaners cannot shift. A heavy-duty degreaser contains stronger surfactants and solvency to penetrate these layers, cutting cleaning time and labour.

Better maintenance visibility

Clean machines show oil leaks, cracks and loose fittings that dirt hides. Many plants find faults early simply because equipment is clean enough to inspect.

Fire risk reduction

Oil-soaked dust near hot surfaces is a fire hazard. Regular degreasing reduces this risk in engine rooms and boiler houses.

Dilution

Dilution is set for soil level.

What a successful clean looks like

Oil-free machine and floor surfaces that are ready for inspection, with less scrubbing time and a lower fire risk around hot equipment.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Workshops, engine rooms and generator bays in Pakistani factories accumulate heavy oil and carbon. A strong degreaser shortens cleaning time during planned maintenance.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Scrape off thick deposits before applying the degreaser.
  • Apply at the recommended dilution and allow dwell time.
  • Agitate with a brush on stubborn areas.
  • Rinse thoroughly and collect the run-off properly.
  • Use gloves and eye protection as per the safety data sheet.
