Streak-free matters

Glass partitions, entrances, display cases and mirrors are the first thing visitors notice. Residue from the wrong cleaner leaves streaks that look dirtier than before.

A dedicated glass and surface cleaner lifts the film and dries without streaks.

Where it fits

It suits daily housekeeping in offices, malls, hotels and factory reception areas. Seamens can supply it as part of a complete housekeeping range.

How Glass & Surface Cleaner works

Streaks are caused by cleaner residue and dissolved minerals drying on glass. A glass cleaner lifts finger marks and dust film and evaporates cleanly so nothing is left behind.

Glass & Surface Cleaner is sprayed on, wiped with a clean microfibre cloth and buffed, giving a clear finish on glass, mirrors and smooth surfaces.

Buyer's guide to Glass & Surface Cleaner

Why ordinary detergents leave streaks

Washing-up liquids and general cleaners contain ingredients that dry as a thin film. On glass, that film scatters light and shows as smears, especially when sunlight falls across a window or a showroom display. A dedicated glass and surface cleaner is formulated to lift dust, fingerprints and light grease and then evaporate cleanly, so the surface dries clear without heavy buffing.

Dust, smog and fingerprints

In Lahore and other large cities, fine dust and smog particles settle on glass every day, while doors, counters and touch panels collect fingerprints and skin oils. A good routine removes both: a light spray, a wipe with a clean microfibre cloth, and a final dry pass. Frequent light cleaning takes less time and chemical than occasional heavy cleaning.

Surfaces it suits

Besides windows and mirrors, the cleaner is used on glass partitions, display cabinets, stainless steel panels, laminated desks and other smooth, non-porous surfaces. For coated, tinted or specialist glass, test on a small area first and follow the advice given for that surface.

Cloths and technique matter

Most streaks come from dirty or fluffy cloths rather than the product. Microfibre cloths, washed without fabric softener and changed often, give the best results. Working from top to bottom and finishing in one direction makes any missed area easy to see.

A professional first impression

Reception areas, meeting rooms and showrooms are where clients and auditors form their first view of a company. Clear, clean glass is a small detail that signals a well-run site, and a reliable product makes it easy for housekeeping staff to keep it that way every day.

Safe, simple use for staff

The product is used by housekeeping teams with basic protective measures as stated on the label and safety data sheet. Clear dilution and use instructions help new staff get consistent results without guesswork.

Indoor air and comfort

A low-residue product used with good ventilation keeps offices pleasant for staff while cleaning.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Dust in Pakistani cities settles quickly on glass. A streak-free cleaner keeps offices and showrooms looking professional.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Use a clean microfibre cloth.
  • Avoid direct sunlight while cleaning.
  • Spray lightly.
  • Wipe in one direction.
  • Change cloths often.
