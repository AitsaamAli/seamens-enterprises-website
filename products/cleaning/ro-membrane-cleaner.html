Signs a membrane needs cleaning

Operators usually clean when normalised permeate flow drops, differential pressure rises or salt passage increases beyond set limits. Cleaning early, before fouling becomes heavy, gives better recovery.

Alkaline cleaners remove organic matter and biofilm; scale is usually removed with a separate acid clean.

Cleaning procedure

Membrane cleaning follows the membrane maker's limits for pH and temperature. Seamens advises on the clean-in-place procedure for your RO or UF system.

How RO Membrane Cleaner works

Organic matter and biofilm cling to RO membranes in a sticky layer. An alkaline cleaner raises pH within the membrane maker's limits, loosening and dissolving organic and biological foulants so they can be flushed away.

Cleaning is done by circulating the solution through the vessels from a clean-in-place tank, soaking if needed, then flushing before returning the plant to service.

Identifying the foulant first

Membrane fouling comes in several forms: organic matter, biofilm, silt and colloids, metal oxides and mineral scale. Alkaline cleaners are most effective on organic, biological and silt fouling, while mineral scale usually needs an acid clean. Reviewing feed water analysis, the stage where performance has dropped and, if possible, a membrane autopsy shows which clean is needed, so chemical and downtime are not wasted.

A typical cleaning sequence

Cleaning normally follows a set order: flush the system with permeate, prepare the cleaning solution at the right pH and temperature, recirculate at low pressure, allow a soak period, recirculate again and then flush thoroughly before returning to service. Cleaning each stage separately often gives better results than cleaning the whole array together.

Protecting membrane warranty

Membrane manufacturers publish pH and temperature limits for cleaning. Staying inside those limits protects both the membranes and any warranty. Seamens sets the cleaning conditions with those limits in mind.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

High-TDS groundwater in many Pakistani cities fouls RO membranes fast. Regular alkaline cleaning extends membrane life and protects the investment.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Clean when normalised flow drops or pressure rises by the agreed amount.
  • Use the correct temperature and pH for your membranes.
  • Recirculate for the recommended time.
  • Flush thoroughly before returning to service.
  • Record performance before and after.
