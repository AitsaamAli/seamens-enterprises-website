What scaling does to an RO plant

As an RO plant pushes water through membranes, the salts left behind become concentrated. At the tail end of the system they can exceed their solubility and deposit on the membrane surface, cutting permeate flow and raising pressure.

An anti-scalant dosed before the membranes keeps those salts from depositing, so the plant holds its output and membranes need cleaning less often.

Dosing from the feed water

The right dose depends on the feed water chemistry and the plant's recovery. Seamens calculates it from a laboratory analysis of your feed water.
