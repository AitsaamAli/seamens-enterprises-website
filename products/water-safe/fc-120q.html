Water Safe FC-120Q: Anti-Scale and Corrosion Inhibitor for Air Humidification Plants

Water Safe FC-120Q is an anti-scale, corrosion inhibitor and de-scaling cleaner for air humidification plants. It de-scales and cleans eliminator plates, nozzles, SS caps, tiles, showering pipes, SS clamps and intake grills, so A/C plants last longer and stay free of scaling, sludge, algae, micro short cotton dust and other industrial problems.

Why humidification plants need protection

An air washer or humidification plant sprays recirculating water through nozzles and draws air across wet eliminator plates. Hardness in the water leaves scale on nozzles and plates, sludge collects in the tank and on the tiles, and warm, wet surfaces encourage algae.

In textile mills the plant pulls in fine cotton fly and micro short cotton dust. That lint sticks to wet scale and sludge, blocks nozzles and intake grills, and cuts both airflow and humidity control.

Protection plus cleaning

FC-120Q has one combined function: it inhibits scale and corrosion in the recirculating water and de-scales and cleans eliminator plates, nozzles, SS caps, tiles, showering pipes, SS clamps and intake grills. Seamens sets the dose and the cleaning routine for your plant.

How Water Safe FC-120Q works

In an air humidification plant, a pump lifts water from the tank to banks of nozzles that spray it into the passing air. Eliminator plates downstream catch the droplets and return them to the tank. Because water keeps evaporating, the minerals it carries concentrate with every hour of running, and they come out as hard scale on nozzles, SS caps, showering pipes and eliminator plates.

Water Safe FC-120Q works on two fronts. Dosed into the recirculating water, it inhibits scale and protects metal parts against corrosion. Used as a cleaning product, it helps de-scale and clean the parts that foul first, so the plant goes back into service with open nozzles, clean plates and clear intake grills.

Benefits of Water Safe FC-120Q in detail

Open, even spray

Nozzles and SS caps free of scale give a fine, even spray and steady humidity in the hall.

Clean eliminator plates

De-scaled plates let air pass freely and catch droplets properly, so water is not carried into ducts.

Less sludge in tanks and on tiles

Cleaner tanks and tiles mean fewer blocked strainers and less manual scraping.

Algae kept down

Clean, scale-free surfaces give algae less to cling to between cleanings.

Cotton dust does not cake

Micro short cotton dust has less wet scale and sludge to stick to, so grills and nozzles stay clearer.

Longer plant life

Inhibiting scale and corrosion protects SS clamps, showering pipes and pump parts for many seasons.

Buyer's guide to Water Safe FC-120Q

Where scale and dust collect first

In an air washer the first parts to foul are the nozzles, SS caps and eliminator plates, followed by the tank tiles and intake grills. Checking these in a fixed order shows quickly whether the plant needs a clean.

A cleaning routine that works

Most plants combine a steady inhibitor dose in the water with planned cleaning stops. During a stop, nozzles, eliminators, showering pipes and grills are de-scaled and rinsed, and the tank is drained and cleaned before refilling.

Nozzle checks

A quick walk past each nozzle bank shows blocked or dripping nozzles before humidity drifts.

Planned cleaning stops

Cleaning on a fixed routine is faster and cheaper than waiting for a breakdown.

Seasonal review

Seamens reviews the dose and the cleaning routine before and after the summer peak.

What a well-kept plant looks like

An even spray from every nozzle, clean eliminator plates, clear intake grills, a clean tank and steady humidity in the hall show that scale, sludge, algae and cotton dust are under control.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Pakistani spinning and weaving mills run humidification plants almost all year, often on hard borehole water and in air loaded with cotton fly. Scale, sludge, algae and micro short cotton dust build up quickly in this climate, so a product that both protects the water and cleans the plant parts saves downtime and keeps humidity steady in production halls.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Inspect nozzles bank by bank and clean any that spray unevenly.
  • De-scale eliminator plates before scale bridges the gaps between blades.
  • Drain, clean and rinse the tank and tiles on a fixed routine.
  • Keep intake grills free of cotton fly so airflow stays steady.
  • Keep the inhibitor dose steady between cleanings.

What does Water Safe FC-120Q do?

Which problems does FC-120Q help control?

Can it be used on stainless steel parts?
Yes. SS caps, SS clamps and showering pipes are among the parts it is used to clean; the routine is confirmed for your plant.
