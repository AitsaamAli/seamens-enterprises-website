Water Safe BR-111: Anti-Scalant for Steam Boilers

Water Safe BR-111 is an anti-scalant for steam boilers. It helps keep hardness minerals from forming scale on boiler tubes, where even a thin layer increases fuel use and raises tube metal temperature.

What scale costs a boiler

Scale is a poor conductor of heat. When it coats the water side of boiler tubes, more fuel is needed to raise the same steam, and the metal behind the scale runs hotter, which can lead to overheating and tube failure.

An anti-scalant is dosed to keep hardness in a form that does not deposit, so it can be removed with blowdown.

Dosed from feed water analysis

The right dose depends on feed water hardness, boiler pressure and blowdown practice. Seamens tests your feed water and boiler water and sets the BR-111 programme for fire-tube or water-tube boilers.

How Water Safe BR-111 works

Feed water carries hardness into the boiler. As water turns to steam, the hardness concentrates and deposits on hot tubes. An anti-scalant changes how these minerals behave so they stay suspended as a removable sludge instead of a hard scale.

Water Safe BR-111 provides that anti-scale protection for fire-tube and water-tube boilers. The dose is set from feed water analysis and boiler pressure, and blowdown removes the conditioned solids.

What good scale control looks like

Clean tubes at the annual inspection, steady stack temperature at a given load, stable fuel use per tonne of steam and boiler water results within the agreed limits show that the anti-scalant programme is working.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Pakistan's textile and food factories rely heavily on gas and coal-fired boilers, and fuel cost is a major line item. Preventing scale keeps heat transfer efficient and fuel bills lower.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Test feed water hardness daily.
  • Keep blow-down regular to control dissolved solids.
  • Inspect tubes during annual shutdown.
  • Record fuel use per tonne of steam.
  • Combine with oxygen scavenging as advised.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Water Safe BR-111 do?
It is an anti-scalant for steam boilers that helps prevent scale on boiler tubes.
How is BR-111 dosed?
From your feed water and boiler water analysis, taking pressure and blowdown into account.
What pack sizes are available?
60, 250 and 1,000 KG packs.
Is BR-111 enough on its own?
It is usually used with an oxygen scavenger such as BR-555 as part of a full programme.
Does blowdown matter?
Yes. Blowdown removes the conditioned solids; Seamens helps set the blowdown rate.
Can BR-111 be used in high-pressure boilers?
The programme is set for your boiler type and pressure.
Can BR-111 remove existing scale?
It is an anti-scalant to prevent scale; existing heavy scale may need cleaning first.
How often is dosing checked?
Regularly, alongside boiler water testing.
