Hard water and A/C plants

Much of the groundwater used by industry around Lahore is hard. When that water is heated and concentrated in an A/C plant, calcium and magnesium salts deposit as scale on the tubes.

Reducing hardness lowers the amount of scale-forming mineral in the system, which supports the rest of the treatment programme.

Part of a programme

FC-30B works alongside the other Water Safe A/C plant products. Seamens tests your water and decides where softening fits in your programme.

How Water Safe FC-30B works

Hardness is the calcium and magnesium dissolved in water. When water is heated or concentrated by evaporation, these minerals come out as scale. Reducing hardness reduces the raw material for scale.

Water Safe FC-30B is the softening product in the A/C plant range. It works alongside inhibitors to lower the scale-forming load in systems fed with hard groundwater.

What softening achieves

Water softening exchanges calcium and magnesium for ions that do not form scale. With less hardness in the make-up water, an A/C plant can run more cycles with lower scaling risk.

Keeping the softener effective

Softening performance falls when regeneration or servicing is missed. Regular hardness checks on the outlet show when service is due.

Reducing descaling frequency

Plants that soften make-up water usually need less frequent condenser descaling, saving downtime.

Testing kits

Simple kits let operators check hardness daily.

What good softening looks like

Low outlet hardness on every check, cleaner condenser tubes at inspection and fewer descaling stops through the season show softening is effective.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

Borehole water in many parts of Punjab is hard. For A/C plants using it as make-up, softening the water is often the first and most effective step against scale.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Test make-up hardness before and after softening.
  • Regenerate or service softening equipment on schedule.
  • Combine softening with inhibitor treatment for full protection.
  • Check condenser approach temperatures for scale signs.
  • Keep records of hardness readings for audits.
