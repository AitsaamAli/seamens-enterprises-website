Water Safe FC-7090 (FC-70/90): Organic De-Scaler for Chillers and A/C Plants

Water Safe FC-7090 (FC-70/90) is a two-way action organic de-scaler for chiller and A/C plant descaling. It is safe for copper and aluminium heat exchangers and does not require a system shutdown for dosing.

Descaling without stopping the plant

When scale has already built up, an inhibitor alone will not remove it. Traditional acid descaling usually means shutting the plant down, which is hard to schedule for factories, hospitals and malls that need cooling continuously.

FC-7090 is dosed without a shutdown and works on the scale while the system keeps running.

Certified for CONAIR and Luwa equipment

Seamens Enterprises' water treatment chemicals are certified by CONAIR and Luwa-Nederman for use in their industrial air-handling, humidification and climate-control equipment installed across Pakistan.

How Water Safe FC-7090 (FC-70/90) works

Once scale has formed inside a chiller or A/C heat exchanger, it must be dissolved. FC-7090 is an organic de-scaler with two-way action designed to work while the system keeps running, so cooling does not stop.

Because it is safe for copper and aluminium heat exchangers, it can be used on the mixed-metal equipment common in A/C plants. Seamens plans the dosing and monitors the system during descaling.

Descaling is planned for low-load periods to avoid disruption.

What a successful clean looks like

After descaling, condenser approach temperature and head pressure should fall back toward design values, tube inspection should show clean metal, and the chiller should draw less power at the same load. Recording these figures before and after shows the benefit clearly to plant management.

Why it matters for plants in Pakistan

Local context

After a hot Pakistani summer, many chiller condensers are scaled and running inefficiently. An organic descaler that is safe for copper and aluminium allows a clean-up before the next season without risking the tubes.

Practical tips for the best results

  • Isolate and circulate the descaler through the heat exchanger as advised.
  • Monitor the solution during cleaning to judge progress.
  • Flush thoroughly after descaling.
  • Restart with a proper inhibitor programme to stop scale returning.
  • Record approach temperature before and after to show the gain.
