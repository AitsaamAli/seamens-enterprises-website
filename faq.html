Seamens Enterprises is a Lahore-based industrial chemical and engineering company, established in 1997. It formulates and manufactures textile auxiliaries (ECO-Series) and water treatment chemicals (Water Safe) in Pakistan and supplies industrial cleaning chemicals, boilers, cooling plants, machinery parts and plant services.

Seamens Enterprises has operated since 1997, is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001, and is registered with the FBR and the Intellectual Property Organization of Pakistan. Who supplies textile auxiliary chemicals in Lahore?
Seamens Enterprises, on Main Sharqpur Road, Lahore, formulates and manufactures the 17-product ECO-Series covering sizing, pre-treatment, dyeing, softening and specialty finishing, and supplies mills in Lahore, Faisalabad and across Pakistan.
What sizing agents does Seamens Enterprises supply?
The ECO-Series sizing range includes EcoSize-CXW-111, EcoPVA-GS and EcoAcryllic-ACL-55. The right choice depends on your yarn and process and is confirmed by trial.
What softeners does Seamens Enterprises supply?
EcoSoftener D-11 for hand-feel and drapability, EcoSil-SS amino-functional silicone softener for premium finishing, and EcoShort-GS-777 for improved fabric handle.
Does Seamens supply flame retardant or water repellent finishes?

How do I compare textile chemical quotes?
Convert each quote to cost per kilogram of fabric: price per kg multiplied by dose in g/L, multiplied by liquor ratio, divided by 1,000. See our guide to choosing a textile chemical supplier.
Where can I buy boiler water treatment chemicals in Lahore?
Seamens Enterprises supplies Water Safe Boiler Treatment, which provides oxygen scavenging, scale inhibition, pH conditioning and sludge dispersal for fire-tube and water-tube boilers, with on-site analysis and monitoring visits.
Does Seamens Enterprises supply steam boilers?
Yes. Fire-tube boilers from 0.5 to 25 ton/hr, water-tube boilers for high pressure and thermic fluid heaters, with boiler room erection, piping and annual maintenance.
What TDS does Lahore groundwater have?
Lahore groundwater commonly runs about 800 to 1,500 ppm TDS, so boiler and cooling programmes should be set from a water analysis rather than a generic dose.
Does Seamens support air humidification in textile mills?
Yes. Seamens supports humidity control with high-pressure fogging systems, ultrasonic humidifiers, evaporative cooling pads and sensors, and its water treatment chemicals are certified by CONAIR and Luwa-Nederman for use in their equipment in Pakistan.
Who supplies cooling tower chemicals in Lahore?
Seamens Enterprises supplies Water Safe-1020, a cooling tower dispersant for scale and biological fouling control, plus Cooling Tower Descaler and biocide programmes, with on-site analysis.
What is used to descale chillers without shutdown?
Water Safe FC-70/90 is a two-way action organic de-scaler for chillers and A/C plants, safe for copper and aluminium heat exchangers, and does not require system shutdown for dosing.
Which RO antiscalant does Seamens supply?
Water Safe RO Antiscalant prevents membrane fouling and scaling in RO systems. Seamens also supplies RO Membrane Cleaner and RO plants with UF pre-treatment and ZLD systems.
What chiller capacities does Seamens supply?
Screw chillers from 50 to 2,000 TR and centrifugal chillers, air-cooled and water-cooled, plus cold storage system design.
Where can I buy industrial cleaning chemicals in Lahore?
Seamens Enterprises supplies Heavy Duty Degreaser, Alkaline CIP Cleaner, Textile Machine Cleaner, Metal Passivator, disinfectants, washroom, floor and glass cleaners and drain unclogger, with free trial samples.
Does Seamens supply textile machinery spare parts?
Yes. Spare parts for weaving, spinning, knitting and dyeing machinery, plus pump, compressor, heat exchanger and valve parts, sourced from Germany, Italy and China.
Does Seamens offer plant erection and maintenance?

How do I choose a reliable chemical supplier in Pakistan?
Verify registration numbers, check the ISO 9001 certificate on IAF CertSearch, request data sheets, call references, run a trial on your own equipment and compare cost-in-use rather than price per kilogram. Our supplier guide and scorecard walk through each step.
Who is the best chemical supplier in Pakistan?
There is no single best supplier for every plant. The right supplier is the one that passes verifiable checks on your own process. Seamens Enterprises welcomes the same checks and offers free trials so you can judge results on your own equipment.
